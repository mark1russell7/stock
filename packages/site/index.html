Stock with due dates: lots of items, due rules as data, first expiring first out, rotation dates and an append-only log.

Try it: a sample pantry

This demo keeps a sample pantry as an append-only log in your browser. Each view below is a fold of the log at the date that you select. The sample is for 2 people at 2,000 calories and 1 gallon of water each day.
